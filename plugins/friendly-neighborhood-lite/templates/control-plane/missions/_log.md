# Mission Log

Running record of completed missions, newest first. Written at completion by the `mission` skill, immediately before the mission folder is deleted.

**This is the durable record.** The mission folder does not survive completion, so anything not carried into an entry here (or rescued to `outputs/`, the vault, or a project) is gone. Keep entries proportional — a mission is the lightest tier and does not get workstream-grade distillation — but do not leave an entry so thin that the work becomes unreconstructable.

Each entry carries four things at most: what the job **was**, what it actually **produced**, what is **carried forward**, and a **handoff** if another piece of work picks up from here. Omit any that do not apply. If a mission taught nothing worth repeating, say so rather than inventing something.

**Read it for repeats.** A lesson appearing twice has outgrown this log and belongs somewhere more permanent — a skill, a tool, or a checklist.
